Weekly Balance Planner
This is an educational trainer for university professors designed to explore time-balance across five life categories. It works with one abstract week — no real dates, no overlapping events — so you can focus entirely on the balance between types of activity, not on scheduling logistics.

The goal is not to make all indicators green or to achieve a "perfect" week. Instead, pay attention to:
  • how different commitments compete for limited time
  • what you tend to protect first when overload increases
  • what gets moved, reduced or sacrificed
  • how much flexible and unallocated time remains for recovery and the unexpected
1
Schedule 1 — Illustrative balanced reference. Observe how time is distributed. Is this pattern relevant to your own situation?
2
Schedule 2 — Moderate overload. Work (non-locked) events to restore balance. Drag to move, resize from the bottom edge, or click to edit.
3
Schedule 3 — Critical overload. Major replanning required. What would you protect? What would you sacrifice? What patterns do you notice?
🔒 Locked events represent fixed institutional commitments that cannot be moved (e.g. scheduled lectures, mandatory meetings).
+ Add event — click any empty slot in the calendar to add your own event.
Import / Export XML lets you save your edited schedule as a file and reload it later — useful for sharing your plan or continuing work between sessions. This tool works with one abstract week and is not a full calendar application.
